# Book-authorship recognition — certificate and translation

[English](https://github.com/FarlandGame/Visual-Animation-Tutorial/blob/main/docs/recognition.en.md) · [فارسی](https://github.com/FarlandGame/Visual-Animation-Tutorial/blob/main/docs/recognition.fa.md) · [日本語](https://github.com/FarlandGame/Visual-Animation-Tutorial/blob/main/docs/recognition.ja.md)

I received a certificate of appreciation from the Maragheh Education Administration for authoring Visual Animation Tutorial. The photographed certificate names Bardiya Alizadeh and is dated 1404/09/09 (Solar Hijri).

The recipient supplied this photograph. The Persian original is the source; the English text and image are unofficial translations prepared for this portfolio. The unclear reference number is not reconstructed.

## Unofficial English translation

In the name of the Most High

**Certificate of Appreciation for Authoring a Book**

“Nūn. By the pen and what they write.” — Qur’an, Surah Al-Qalam, verse 1

**Dear Mr Bardiya Alizadeh,**

Peace be upon you.

Books are a gateway to the vast world of knowledge and understanding, and providing good books is one of the best means of helping people fulfil their potential.

We hereby express our sincere thanks and appreciation for your tireless and enduring efforts in authoring the valuable book **Visual Animation Tutorial** (*آموزش انیمیشن سازی تصویری*). We ask Almighty God to grant you honour and ever-growing success throughout your life.

**Ali Narimani**

Deputy Director-General and Director of Education, Maragheh

**Date:** 1404/09/09 (Solar Hijri)

**Reference number:** see original photograph.

The source photograph is retained unchanged. The viewing image, where provided, corrects framing and lighting; it is not an official digital reissue. The English display is clearly marked as an unofficial translation and uses a typed signatory name rather than reproducing a signature.
